User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Overview

According to the Facebook Platform and Meta Developer rules, users have the right to request the deletion of their personal data collected through Ride To Dream. If you want to delete your activities or data associated with our application, follow the simple instructions below.

Option 1: Remove via Facebook / Instagram Settings

  1. Log in to your Facebook account and go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. In the left menu, select Business Integrations or Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Ride To Dream in the list of authorized applications.
  4. Click the Remove button.
  5. Check the box to delete all posts, videos, or events published on your behalf, and click Remove to confirm.

Once removed, Meta automatically invalidates all access tokens and sends a data deauthorization event to our servers, which immediately wipes all associated token records.

Option 2: Direct Data Deletion Request

You can also request immediate manual deletion of all your stored data, account identifiers, and publishing logs from our databases:

Send an email to: dreamtoride108@gmail.com

Subject: Ride To Dream Data Deletion Request

Include: Your Instagram username (e.g., @dreamtoride1082026) or Facebook User ID.

Upon receipt of your request, our team will purge all database records, access tokens, and cached media within 24 business hours and send you a written confirmation.
