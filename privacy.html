Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ride To Dream ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in full compliance with global privacy regulations, including GDPR, CCPA, and Meta's Developer Policies. This Privacy Policy explains how our platform collects, processes, uses, and safeguards information when you connect your Instagram account or use our video creation and scheduling services.

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize Ride To Dream via Meta Facebook Login or Instagram Login, we only access the minimal data required to publish and manage your authorized content:

  • Instagram Account Information: Your Instagram username, Instagram Business/Creator Account ID, and profile picture URL.
  • Facebook Page Information: Page name, Page ID, and linked Instagram connection status to enable official Content Publishing API access.
  • Authentication Tokens: Temporary OAuth access tokens provided by Meta to authenticate API requests on your behalf. These tokens are stored encrypted and never exposed.
  • Media Content: Video files, thumbnail images, captions, and hashtags that you explicitly create, approve, and queue for publishing.

What We Do NOT Collect: We never collect or store your Instagram or Facebook password, personal direct messages, private contacts, credit card information, or biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To upload and publish short-form video Reels and posts to your authorized Instagram profile via Meta's official Content Publishing API.
  • To check the transcoding and publishing status of video media containers created on Instagram servers.
  • To display post analytics, permalinks, and publication audit logs within your creator dashboard.
  • To ensure compliance with Instagram community standards and publishing rate limits.

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or connected account data to third parties or data brokers under any circumstances.

Data is shared solely with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: In order to deliver API requests to publish content, your media files and access tokens are transmitted securely over TLS/HTTPS directly to Meta Graph API endpoints.
  • Hosting & Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud storage providers used temporarily to host video container URLs required by Meta's API ingestion servers.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain access tokens and account references only for as long as your account remains connected to Ride To Dream. Media files uploaded to temporary staging CDN bridges are automatically purged within 24 to 48 hours following successful publication on Instagram.

All stored access credentials are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption and transmitted exclusively over HTTPS/TLS.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Instructions

You maintain complete control over your data. You may at any time:

  • Revoke our application's access via your Facebook Account Settings under Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations → Ride To Dream → Remove.
  • Disconnect your Instagram account directly from the Ride To Dream dashboard.
  • Request immediate deletion of all account data and tokens by visiting our Data Deletion Instructions Page or emailing us at dreamtoride108@gmail.com.
